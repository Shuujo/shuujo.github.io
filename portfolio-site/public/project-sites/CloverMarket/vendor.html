Information

How are vendors selected?

We go through all applications to ensure they have followed all needed requirements within what's called phase 1.

If they have been rejected, they will receive an email specifying why they have been turned down. Anyone who’s been rejected during phase 1 can request revision on their applications. By contacting CloverMarket.team@gmail.ca with the subject “Revision Request”.

Within Phase 2:
70% will be chosen by the jury in the selection process.
30% will be selected through a lottery system for those who weren't selected

Email will be sent out regardless, notifying of acceptance and or turn down.
What to expect when accepted? Additional information on set up time, deposit and or more will be emailed alongside.




Requirements

Here at Clover Market we absolutely will not tolerate any type of form of use of AI. Any application that may contain a form of AI will immediately be rejected from consideration.

As well, any mass-produced/low-effort “3D Slop” will also be immediately rejected from consideration due to the inflation amount in the marketspace.

Lastly, any mass drop shipping will also be declined from being a participating member.

If you have received the rejection due to the possible AI and or drop shipping you may request a revision of the application.

If we have determined that AI and or drop shipping was in use on the day of market date, we ask you to immediately leave the premises with a full refund of your payment. A full ban will be placed onto the business, which will no longer welcome ever participating in Clover Market in the future.




Summary / tl;dr

  • No AI
  • No "3D Slop"
  • No Drop Shipped Items

FAQs

What does half table mean?

It is a great cheap alternative to the full table price! It's when you share a half of a 4x6 table with a fellow vendor. You may request which vendor if you know someone or we select one for you!

What to do if I've been rejected for breaking the requirements, even though I haven't?

If you have received the rejection due to the possible AI and or drop shipping you may request a revision of the application. Contact CloverMarket.team@gmail.ca with the subject “Revision Request”. With revision, a request of evidence will be requested to determine reconsideration.

What is considered during jury selection?

60% first timer
40% returning
(These percentages may change based on the balance of application and are adjusted to aim for the similar to the intended goal.)
