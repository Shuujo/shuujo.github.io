Information

Requirements

  • We require at least 16 of age to be fully considered
  • No experience is needed nor a resume! Feel free to drop a resume, however this does not affect your application!
  • Hours of volunteering range from 2-8 hours with breaks!



Warning

Any form of disrespectful/discriminating behaviour, will act in immediate warning. If reported/found once again we asked you to leave the premises with a full ban placed onto the named person, which will no longer welcome ever participating in Clover Market in the future.




Roles

Main Assisting

  • Providing directions to Market
  • Helping attendees of basic questions
  • Provide a helping hand to setups/take downs
  • Manage booth, if vendors are needed washroom break

Information booth

  • Inform attendees of Clover Market mission
  • Inform attendees of locations of booths
  • Inform attendees of performance schedule
  • Inform attendees of Washroom

Crowd Control

  • Help control crowds during performance and ensure everyone's safety!



FAQs

Do I need any experience? Or a resume?

No experience is needed nor a resume! Feel free to drop a resume, however this does not affect your application!

How old do I need to be to apply?

We require at least 16 of age to be fully considered.

How long is volunteering?

Volunteering requires a 3-hour training session one day prior to the event day. On event day, hours of volunteering range from 2-8 hours with breaks!
